Formal Logic Proofs

1. Modus Ponens (Emergency Priority)

Premise 1: If an emergency vehicle is detected (E), then the signal must turn GREEN (G).
(E → G)

Premise 2: An emergency vehicle is detected.
(E)

Conclusion: Therefore, the signal must turn GREEN.
(∴ G)

2. Modus Tollens (Accident/Maintenance Stop)

Premise 1: If the signal is allowed to turn GREEN (G), then there is no accident reported (~A).
(G → ~A)

Premise 2: There is an accident reported (A).
(~(~A))

Conclusion: Therefore, the signal is not allowed to turn GREEN.
(∴ ~G)
